## Fushi 3.0.0

- **Interface refresh**: Material and Cupertino designs, smoother interactions, reorderable modules and a selectable startup page; paged bottom navigation keeps lookup pinned at the bottom right
- **Reader & manga**: page-turn animations, customizable highlights, improved position restoration and whole-volume OCR; experimental foldable layouts for dual-page reading and tabletop video playback
- **Read-aloud & Audiobook Studio**: multiple voices, sentence highlighting, AI speaker annotation and M4B export; Audiobookshelf and automatic transcription; Windows gains in-app Irodori-TTS and GPT-SoVITS deployment
- **Advanced mining**: adjust deck, context, audio boundaries, video frames and manga crops; mosaic card images and use configured TTS voices for missing word audio; improved Anki video-template compatibility
- **Video & subtitles**: bitmap subtitle OCR, optional pause OCR, phone control for episodes, volume, subtitle tracks and mining; Windows HDR, Blu-ray menus and Dolby / DTS passthrough
- **Collections & downloads**: automatic series grouping, batch actions and AI volume ordering; find a work's download tasks and optionally delete files when clearing completed jobs
- **AI & models**: unified model settings, multiple API keys, rate controls and retries; improved automatic subtitles with Parakeet / Kitsune models and on-demand Windows GPU acceleration
- **Sync & desktop**: fewer duplicate transfers, playback-progress conflict checks, recovery for removed remote books, and a fix for deleted books reappearing as remote placeholders; desktop screen OCR and `fushi_cli`
- **Games**: experimental stream reception expands to iOS, macOS and Windows; more Windows galgame adapters. Streaming hosts and local hooking remain Windows-only
- **Feedback & statistics**: reports with logs, screenshots and video, reply notifications and fix-release tracking; custom date ranges and per-title statistics
- **Accounts & leaderboard**: sign in through a paired owner device; faster cover processing, confirmed cover votes with replacement safeguards, and improved email-alias handling
- **Fixes & performance**: reduce Windows video UI stalls; improve subtitle timing, card audio, reading layout and sync; extension auto-updates, browser-overlay fixes and screenshot regression checks

### Notes

- Foldable layouts and in-app GPT-SoVITS deployment are experimental
- The video "pause at subtitle end" mode has been removed
- Very old Hibiki databases must first be opened with **2.9.1** before upgrading
- iOS continues to exclude online extension sources, discovery and the general download center

## Fushi 3.0.0 更新公告

- **界面焕新**：升级 Material 与 Cupertino 设计，完善动效；模块可排序、可选启动页，分页底栏保留右下角固定查词按钮
- **阅读与漫画**：新增翻页动效、自定义高亮，改善位置恢复与整卷 OCR；新增折叠屏双页阅读、半折视频播放布局（实验性）
- **朗读与有声书制作台**：多声音、逐句高亮、AI 说话人标注与 M4B 导出；接入 Audiobookshelf 和自动转录，Windows 支持应用内部署 Irodori-TTS 与 GPT-SoVITS
- **高级制卡**：调整牌组、上下文、音频起止、视频静帧与漫画裁图，支持图片打码、自选 TTS 声音补全单词音频，改善 Anki 视频模板兼容
- **视频与字幕**：图形字幕 OCR、可选暂停识别，手机可遥控选集、音量、字幕轨与制卡；新增 Windows HDR、蓝光菜单和 Dolby / DTS 直通
- **合集与下载**：本地内容自动归组、批量操作与 AI 卷序排序；可定位作品下载任务，清理已完成任务时可选删除文件
- **AI 与模型**：统一模型设置，支持多 Key、限速与重试；自动字幕接入 Parakeet / Kitsune 模型，Windows 可按需下载 GPU 加速组件
- **同步与桌面**：减少重复传输、检查进度冲突，支持远程书籍恢复，修复删除的书以远端占位卡重新出现；新增桌面屏幕 OCR 与 `fushi_cli`
- **游戏**：实验性串流接收扩展至 iOS、macOS 与 Windows，补充 Windows Galgame 适配；串流主机与本机 Hook 仍仅限 Windows
- **反馈与统计**：反馈可附日志、截图与视频，支持回复通知和修复版本追踪；统计支持自定义日期与逐作品明细
- **账号与排行榜**：支持通过已配对的本人设备登录，封面处理提速，完善封面确认投票、替换保护与邮箱别名处理
- **修复与优化**：减少 Windows 视频界面卡顿，改善字幕时间轴、卡片音频、阅读排版与同步；支持扩展自动更新，修复浏览器覆盖层并完善界面截图回归检查

### 注意事项

- 折叠屏布局、应用内部署 GPT-SoVITS 属实验性
- 已移除视频「字幕暂停播放模式」
- 极旧的 Hibiki 数据库需先用 **2.9.1** 打开一次，再升级
- iOS 仍不包含在线扩展源、发现与通用下载中心
