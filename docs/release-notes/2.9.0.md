## Fushi 2.9.0

### Browse & Online Sources
- The Downloads module becomes **Browse**, Mihon-style: Sources / Extensions / Discover / Downloads, each switchable between books, manga and video; libraries keep Discover / Sources / Extensions as their own sub-tabs
- Online video sources: Aniyomi extensions run on the same extension system as manga — browse, play with server switching, add episodes to the library, download, and mine cards from them (not on iOS)
- Online novel sources: LNReader plugins — read online, or download a chapter range as an EPUB with table of contents, cover and illustrations (Android / Windows / macOS; not on iOS)
- Online manga chapters can be read directly, honour chapter skip rules and prefetch the next chapter
- Drag a `.torrent` onto the video, book, manga or download page to add it as a task; torrent packs queue their later volumes instead of failing

### AI
- AI download: say what you want and it identifies the work, then fetches the whole series — every season plus the movies, subscribing to anything still airing; works for video, books, manga and games
- Web references for identification: Wikipedia, Moegirlpedia, Anime News Network, TVmaze and any MediaWiki site you add
- A phone can hand the whole AI download session to a paired computer or Fushi Server
- AI also writes dictionary CSS, Lapis card styles and custom themes, and resolves ambiguous video scrape matches
- Default AI provider, quality / source / bitrate preferences, subtitle languages expanded to 21

### Video
- Media servers: Jellyfin / Emby browsed as their own server tree, **Plex** support, multiple routes per server, landscape continue-watching cards
- More sources: AList / OpenList sites, cloud-drive streaming without downloading, STRM files and M3U / IPTV playlists, Blu-ray (BDMV) discs, and plain audio files played as cover-only videos
- Scraping now follows Shoko: AniDB is the default primary source with file-hash identity; multi-series and movie folders are split automatically; TMDB alternative orderings; "pin season / episode" by hand; poster / backdrop counts; fuller cast lists; works that stay unmatched tell you why
- Subtitles: SubDL added; downloaded subtitles auto-align to the video's embedded track, with manual align / restore; waveform alignment works on every platform
- Player: borderless mini window, screenshot shortcuts with or without subtitles, overflowing controls fold into a "More" menu, brightness / volume swipes can be turned off, hover lookup resumes playback when you leave
- ASS rendering fixes (drawings, layers, shear, outlines), Dolby Vision profile 5 colours, auto-resume after returning from background
- Drop video files onto a collection to import into it; "clear watch progress" and "rescrape metadata and cover" on video cards

### Interconnect & Fushi Server
- Reach your host from anywhere: hosts publish all their addresses (LAN, IPv6, public), clients pick the fastest route, and a P2P tunnel takes over when no direct path works
- Pair by QR code, pasted link, `fushi://pair` deep link or NFC tag
- Weak network: the host transcodes to adaptive HLS on demand
- A desktop app can download on behalf of a phone ("download device"); interconnect downloads keep going in the background and resume
- Tags and collections stay consistent across devices; Hoshi Reader backups can be imported (books, positions, statistics)
- Fushi Server: AI download sessions, novel / manga / audiobook downloads, local audio libraries, profile transfer hub, resource indexer editing, public address / P2P / relay settings in the WebUI, and scan reconciliation for vanished files

### Card Creation & Anki
- Cards are queued when Anki is unreachable and delivered later — across devices through interconnect, or straight to AnkiWeb / a self-hosted sync server by the built-in Anki sync client, no Anki install needed
- Favorites keep their definitions and context; create cards from favorites in batches
- Online video cards come from the player's own buffer, can be made in the background, or saved to mine after watching
- Video clip cover is now a real video clip by default instead of a GIF
- New `{glossary-first-2}` / `{glossary-first-3}` fields, Lapis first-line indent, glossary HTML exported in Yomitan's shape, much faster media de-duplication
- Audiobook sentence audio follows the playback speed (toggle); Bilibili and Emby / Jellyfin cards keep their audio

### Reader
- Floating ball: a Fushi bubble with your reader buttons, the study timer and a "hide toolbars" switch; also available app-wide, system-wide on Android and outside the app on Windows / macOS, with photo lookup (camera → OCR → tap a word)
- Reading settings drawer grouped into tabs; top bar shows the current chapter; pause / resume the study timer with P
- Smooth mouse wheel across the app, stepless wheel and phone-like trackpad scrolling in scroll mode
- Furigana and audiobook highlight fixes on iOS / macOS across paged, scroll and visual-novel modes; the last page of a chapter is reachable again
- Illustration gallery groups by table of contents and adapts thumbnails to window width
- Shelf: reading status filter; finished books leave the continue-reading hero

### Manga
- Reader settings aligned with Mihon: spread offset, wide pages on their own screen, page slider, background colour, double-tap zoom, tap-zone layouts, trackpad pinch zoom
- Panel-by-panel navigation and Mokuro sidecar support
- OCR: whole-volume recognition on open, OCR as you read, re-recognize or switch engine per volume
- New local OCR models: per-column CTC recognizer and a KV-cache speed-up pack for the classic model; GPU (CUDA) recognition on Windows; vertical text through Apple Vision on iOS / macOS
- Models are picked in the engine list, download in the background and can be managed per model

### Windows Galgame
- Support for more games

### Game Streaming (new, experimental)
- Stream a Windows game to an Android device over interconnect: remote launch, window-only capture, touch and gamepad input, Moonlight-style settings and a weak-network resolution ladder
- Look up words and create cards from the streamed game's text and voice lines

### Dictionaries & Lookup
- English: irregular forms and phrasal verbs (gave up → give up) deinflect, even with Yomitan dictionaries without part-of-speech tags
- macOS gets the app-external global lookup overlay; the lookup box switches to your target language's input method
- One-click update of all dictionaries from their remote index
- Pitch accents from several dictionaries merge into one row with an "N dictionaries" label; bottom-docked popup can be toggled per module
- Font library with Japanese specimens, live previews and a system font browser, reachable from dictionary and subtitle settings

### Statistics & Leaderboard
- **Fushi account & leaderboard**: email-verified account, rankings, public shelf, friends, and shareable week / month / all-time cards — in a new Rank tab of the statistics center
- Statistics are kept per profile; selectable time range, covers in by-media lists, landscape two-pane layout, "today" reset time in the header
- Background audiobook playback and browser-extension video watching count toward study time

### Browser Extension
- Subtitle appearance editor with themes that can follow Fushi, a font dropdown with one-click Fushi font download, and a resizable, draggable overlay
- A Fushi button inside the site's own player controls
- Hover lookup resumes playback when you leave; the popup follows the app's dictionary font; Shift-hover lookup can be disabled
- Web video watching counts as immersion time, with options for what counts

### Interface & Stability
- Long-press the app icon for shortcuts on Android and iOS
- "Also delete statistics" works the same for books, manga, video and games
- Desktop settings and built-in themes refreshed to match Material 3; e-ink mode reaches the home page
- Windows: update-notification and accessibility crashes fixed at the root; macOS keeps accessibility permissions across updates
- iOS: online video and novel sources and the Discover tabs are not included, for App Store compliance

## Fushi 2.9.0 更新公告

### 浏览与在线来源
- 「下载」模块改为 Mihon 式的**「浏览」**：来源 / 扩展 / 发现 / 下载四个页签，页签内可切书、漫画、视频；各库页也保留发现 / 来源 / 扩展子标签
- 在线视频源：Aniyomi 扩展与漫画共用同一套扩展系统——浏览、播放并切换线路、加入媒体库、下载、制卡（iOS 版不含）
- 在线小说源：LNReader 插件——在线阅读，或按章节范围下载成带目录、封面与插图的 EPUB（Android / Windows / macOS；iOS 版不含）
- 在线漫画可直接在线读，遵守章节跳过规则并预取下一话
- `.torrent` 可直接拖进视频页、书架、漫画库或下载页添加任务；同一种子包的后续卷改为排队而不是报错

### AI
- AI 下载：说出想要什么，它先识别作品，再整套拉取——全部季加剧场版，在播的自动订阅；视频、书、漫画、游戏都能用
- 识别作品可参考联网资料：维基百科、萌娘百科、Anime News Network、TVmaze 以及自定义 MediaWiki 站点
- 手机可把整场 AI 下载交给已配对的电脑或 Fushi 服务端执行
- AI 还能生成词典 CSS、Lapis 卡片样式和自定义主题，并消解视频刮削的歧义匹配
- 默认 AI 提供商；下载画质 / 片源 / 码率偏好；字幕语言扩到 21 种

### 视频
- 媒体服务器：Jellyfin / Emby 按服务器目录树浏览的独立分区，新增 **Plex**，单服务器多线路可切换，横版「继续观看」卡
- 更多来源：AList / OpenList 站点、网盘视频免下载直接播、STRM 文件与 M3U / IPTV 列表、蓝光原盘（BDMV），纯音频文件也能以封面形式在媒体库播放
- 刮削对齐 Shoko：默认主源改为 AniDB 并用文件哈希认身份；多系列、多剧场版的文件夹自动拆开；支持 TMDB 备选排序；可手动钉死季集；海报 / 背景张数可调；演职员表补全；未匹配的作品会说明原因
- 字幕：接入 SubDL；下载的字幕自动按视频内嵌字幕轨对时间轴，可手动对齐 / 恢复；波形对轴五端可用
- 播放器：无边框小窗；带字幕 / 不带字幕两个截图快捷键；控制栏放不下的按钮收进「更多」菜单；亮度 / 音量滑动手势可关；悬停查词离开即续播
- ASS 排版修正（绘图、图层、切变、描边）；杜比视界 Profile 5 色彩正确；切后台回来自动续播
- 视频文件可拖进合集详情页导入该合集；视频卡新增「清除观看进度」与「重新刮削资料与封面」

### 互联与 Fushi 服务端
- 在外也能连回主机：主机公布全部地址（局域网 / IPv6 / 公网），客户端自动挑最快的一条，直连全失败时走 P2P 隧道兜底
- 配对方式：扫码、粘贴链接、`fushi://pair` 深链、NFC 贴纸
- 弱网可用：主机按需转码成自适应 HLS
- 电脑端 app 可替手机代下载（「下载执行设备」）；互联下载可后台进行、断点续传
- 标签与合集跨设备保持一致；可导入 Hoshi Reader 备份（书、阅读位置、统计）
- Fushi 服务端：承接 AI 下载会话；代下载小说 / 漫画 / 有声书；托管本地音频库；作为 Profile 中转站；WebUI 可编辑资源索引器、公网地址、P2P 与自建中继；扫描时回收已消失的文件

### 制卡与 Anki
- Anki 不在线时卡片先进待发队列，之后补发——可经互联转给其他设备，也可由内置的 Anki 同步客户端直接同步到 AnkiWeb / 自建同步服务器，无需安装 Anki
- 收藏保留释义与上下文，可从收藏批量制卡
- 在线视频制卡直接取播放器缓冲；可后台制卡，也可先记下、看完再制
- 视频片段封面默认改为真正的视频片段，不再是 GIF
- 新增 `{glossary-first-2}` / `{glossary-first-3}` 字段；Lapis 支持首行缩进；释义 HTML 按 Yomitan 形态导出；媒体查重大幅提速
- 有声书句子音频可跟随播放倍速（可开关）；B 站与 Emby / Jellyfin 制卡不再丢音频

### 阅读器
- 悬浮球：Fushi 图标球收纳阅读器按钮、学习计时和「隐藏工具栏」开关；也可全应用使用、在 Android 上系统级悬浮、在 Windows / macOS 上悬浮于应用之外，支持拍照查词（拍照 → OCR → 点词）
- 阅读设置抽屉按标签页分组；顶栏显示当前章名；P 键暂停 / 继续学习计时
- 全应用平滑滚轮；滚动模式下滚轮无级滚动、触控板手感接近手机
- iOS / macOS 上振假名与有声书高亮在翻页、滚动、视觉小说三种模式下的多处修正；章末最后一页重新可达
- 插图册按目录分节，缩略图随窗口宽度自适应
- 书架：按阅读状态筛选；已读完的书不再出现在「继续阅读」大卡

### 漫画
- 阅读设置对齐 Mihon：跨页偏移、宽页独占一屏、跳页滑块、底色、双击缩放、点击热区布局、触控板双指缩放
- 分镜逐格导航；支持 Mokuro sidecar
- OCR：打开即整卷识别、边看边识别、每卷可重新识别或换引擎
- 新本地 OCR 模型：逐列 CTC 识别器、经典模型的 KV 缓存提速包；Windows 支持 GPU（CUDA）识别；iOS / macOS 经 Apple Vision 识别竖排
- 模型在引擎列表里直接选，后台下载，按模型管理存储

### Windows Galgame
- 适配了更多游戏

### 游戏串流（新增，实验性）
- 经互联把 Windows 上的游戏串流到 Android：远程启动、只捕获游戏窗口、触屏与手柄输入、Moonlight 式设置、弱网分辨率阶梯
- 串流中可对游戏台词查词，并带台词语音制卡

### 词典与查词
- 英语查词支持不规则变形与短语动词（gave up → give up），无词性标注的 Yomitan 词典同样生效
- macOS 新增 app 外全局查词浮窗；查词输入框自动切到学习语言的输入法
- 词典可从远端索引一键全部更新
- 多部词典的音调合并成一行并标「N 部词典」；底部停靠弹窗可按模块开关
- 字体库：日文样张、按用途实时预览、系统字体浏览器，可从词典与字幕设置直达

### 统计与排行榜
- **Fushi 账户与排行榜**：邮箱验证注册、排名、公开书架、好友，周 / 月 / 全部时段的分享卡——位于统计中心新增的「排行」页签
- 统计按 Profile 隔离；可选时间范围；按媒体列表显示封面；横屏双栏布局；「今日」重置时刻移到页头
- 后台播放有声书、浏览器扩展里看视频都计入学习时长

### 浏览器扩展
- 字幕外观编辑器，配色主题可跟随 Fushi；字体下拉框并可一键下载 Fushi 字体库；字幕覆盖层可拖动、可拖角改大小
- 站点播放器控制栏里出现 Fushi 按钮
- 悬停查词离开即续播；查词弹窗跟随 app 的词典字体；Shift 悬停查词可关闭
- 网页看视频计入沉浸时间，并可选择什么情况才算入统计

### 界面与稳定性
- Android 与 iOS 长按 app 图标出现快捷入口
- 书、漫画、视频、游戏的删除统一提供「同时删除统计」
- 桌面设置页与内置主题按 Material 3 重整；墨水屏模式覆盖到首页
- Windows：根治更新通知与无障碍相关的崩溃；macOS 更新后不再丢失辅助功能授权
- iOS：按 App Store 要求不含在线视频源、在线小说源与各「发现」页签
