## Fushi 2.10.0

Changes since 2.9.1: a redesigned interface, built-in review, read-aloud and audiobook creation, more flexible card creation, and phone control for desktop video playback.

### Interface & Navigation

- The Material interface moves to **Material 3 Expressive (M3E)**, with refreshed navigation, settings, libraries, lookup popups and player panels, plus spring transitions and press / hover feedback
- The Apple design option is now named **Cupertino**, with platform-style controls and layouts; Windows 11 gains a Mica window backdrop
- Toolbar actions stay visible while space allows and move into overflow by priority on smaller screens
- Reorder navigation modules; choose a flat or swipe-paged mobile bottom bar, with press-and-drag selection in paged mode
- Home is rebuilt around continuing your current book or video; Home and AI can each be turned off in module settings
- Settings gain grouped search results, section shortcuts and page-level reset to defaults

### Built-in Review (new, off by default)

- Enable **Review** under **Settings → Appearance → Modules** to study cards inside Fushi
- Decks, card review, note editing and browsing, with FSRS scheduling and automatic parameter optimization
- Send mined cards to Anki, Fushi Review, or both; import cards from mining history
- Import Anki `.apkg` / `.colpkg` packages and export decks; lookup popups show review status
- Review data and media join sync and backup; a sync-conflict page lets you inspect conflicting edits
- AnkiConnect-compatible actions are available through Fushi API while Review is enabled

### Read-aloud & Audiobook Creation

- Read books aloud with sentence highlighting, switch voices during playback, and use lyrics mode
- Voice presets for GPT-SoVITS, Irodori, Edge, Azure, Google Cloud and other services
- **Audiobook Studio**: choose chapters, assign voices, queue generation, check the result with ASR, then import it or export an M4B
- AI-assisted speaker annotation and multi-voice casting, with pause and loudness controls
- Windows gains an in-app GPT-SoVITS download, installation and local-service manager (experimental; the full installation flow still needs real-device validation)
- Connect to your Audiobookshelf server; downloaded audio can be transcribed and imported automatically, with transcription tasks shown in Downloads
- Floating audiobook subtitles gain multiple sentences, vertical writing and appearance controls; lyrics mode can show book illustrations and swap the player / text sides in wide layouts

### Card Creation & Anki

- **Advanced mining** lets you adjust a single card before sending it: deck, sentence context, audio boundaries and image selection where supported
- Video cards gain precise clip preview and still-frame selection; manga cards can crop the image to the sentence's panel
- Change the mining profile or deck from the lookup popup
- Adapt compatible Anki note templates for inline video, with a reversible change and guidance when a template needs adaptation
- Desktop Anki can launch with Fushi; clip export is faster, and exported subtitles retain more of their original styling
- Fixes for Kiku template detection, missing clip audio, word-audio playback order and oversized dictionary styling in cards

### Video & Subtitles

- **Bitmap subtitle lookup and OCR** for PGS / VobSub / DVB: recognize the current text or convert a whole track into text subtitles, including supported remote sources
- Optional pause OCR for videos; low-confidence results can be re-read by a configured LLM with context
- **Control desktop video from a paired phone**: browse subtitles, look up words, preview sentence audio, favorite lines and ask the host to create cards
- Windows HDR video renders inside the app compositor, keeping subtitles and controls on the same surface
- Native Blu-ray menu navigation, Dolby / DTS passthrough controls, and Atmos / 5.1 / 7.1 badges
- Search across media servers and choose among versions of an Emby / Jellyfin item
- Redesigned playback settings and subtitle / audio panels; configurable subtitle outline and shadow; gamepad word-by-word lookup for paused sentences
- Import audio CDs as FLAC and accept AIFF audio files
- Fixes for subtitle position / animation timing after seeking, Hi10P decoding choices, hardware-decoded picture edges, Blu-ray playback and external subtitles

### Reader, Manga & Collections

- Page-turn animation options for novels and manga; customizable text-highlight strokes and sweep animation
- Improved reading-position restoration after backgrounding, reopening and audiobook following; fixes for furigana highlights and vertical text
- Reworked audiobook side panel, chapter navigation, reading settings and toolbar layout editor; illustrations open full-size with pinch zoom
- Automatically group local manga volumes, novels and audiobooks into series; the series cover follows the volume being read
- Collection filtering, sorting and batch actions; AI ordering for specials and volumes whose order could not be parsed
- Local manga volume pages gain whole-volume OCR, recognition progress and shelf actions; spread offset and reopening behavior are configurable
- Faster illustration loading and long-chapter opening; fixes for manga OCR rotation and recognition-area coordinates

### AI & Models

- A unified **Models** settings category for LLM, OCR, ASR, TTS and model files
- Multiple API keys per provider, with rotation, cooldown, request-rate controls and retries for transient failures
- Large-series AI downloads walk the franchise in batches instead of cutting off the remainder
- Turning off the AI module hides its entrances and disables AI requests while retaining your configuration

### Sync, Desktop & Game Streaming

- Avoid redundant transfers when synced content has not changed; show transfer rates and manga-page content in sync comparison
- Video and audiobook position conflicts are checked before overwriting progress; removed remote books have a recovery list
- Re-fetch audiobook / video subtitles from a host, download collection members in batches, and view or request host updates where supported
- Open EPUB / PDF / TXT / comic files from outside the desktop app; Windows and macOS system floating balls gain screen OCR
- New **fushi_cli** desktop commands for libraries, playback, lookup, mining, settings, downloads and sync
- Experimental game-stream reception expands from Android to iOS, macOS and Windows, with adjustable text / lookup panels; the host and local galgame hooking remain Windows-only
- More Windows galgame engine adapters, including experimental Kogado Hy and LucaSystem work

### Feedback, Statistics & Browser Extension

- **In-app feedback** with logs, screenshots and video attachments, saved drafts, replies and progress tracking; signed-in users can view their own reports across devices
- Developer-reply notifications, including iOS push; linked fixes show which debug / beta / stable release contains them
- Statistics gain custom date ranges and per-title breakdowns; fixes for chart labels, first-page character counts, and galgame skip / backlog text being counted as read
- Browser extension refreshed with M3E / glass styling; queued YouTube cards can be generated from another tab; fixes for fullscreen subtitles, resizing, favorites and shortcuts

### Upgrade Notes

- Review is **off by default**. Online TTS and AI features require the relevant service configuration
- The video "pause at subtitle end" playback mode has been removed
- Very old Hibiki databases must first be opened with **2.9.1**, then upgraded to 2.10.0; the app will prompt if this applies
- iOS continues to exclude online extension sources, discovery and the general download center; game-stream reception is a separate feature

## Fushi 2.10.0 更新公告

本次汇总 2.9.1 之后的更新：界面全面改版，新增内置复习、书籍朗读与有声书制作台，并带来高级制卡、手机遥控电脑视频等功能。

### 界面与导航

- Material 界面升级为 **Material 3 Expressive（M3E）**：导航、设置、媒体库、查词弹窗与播放面板统一重做，加入弹簧转场、按压与悬停反馈
- Apple 设计选项更名为 **Cupertino**，调整为对应平台的控件与布局；Windows 11 新增 Mica 窗口背景
- 工具栏按钮在空间足够时直接展开，窄屏下按优先级收进「更多」
- 导航模块可拖拽排序；手机底栏可选平铺或分页滑动，分页模式支持按住拖选
- 首页围绕「继续阅读 / 观看」重新整理；首页和 AI 均可在功能模块设置中关闭
- 设置新增分组搜索结果、分区快捷跳转与整页恢复默认

### 内置复习（新增，默认关闭）

- 在 **设置 → 外观 → 功能模块** 开启「复习」，即可在 Fushi 内学习卡片
- 支持牌组管理、卡片复习、笔记编辑与浏览，使用 FSRS 排程并支持自动优化参数
- 制卡目标可选 Anki、Fushi 复习或两者；已有制卡历史可批量导入复习
- 支持导入 Anki `.apkg` / `.colpkg` 包及导出牌组；查词弹窗显示复习状态
- 复习数据与媒体纳入同步和备份，提供同步冲突查看入口
- 开启复习后，Fushi API 可提供兼容 AnkiConnect 的操作

### 书籍朗读与有声书制作

- 阅读器新增 TTS 朗读，支持逐句高亮、播放中切换声音，也可进入歌词模式
- 声音预设支持 GPT-SoVITS、Irodori、Edge、Azure、Google Cloud 等服务
- 新增 **有声书制作台**：选择章节、分配声音、排队生成，用 ASR 检查结果，再入库或导出 M4B
- 支持 AI 辅助标注说话人、多角色配音，以及停顿、响度调整
- Windows 新增应用内 GPT-SoVITS 下载、安装与本机服务管理（实验性，完整安装链路仍待真机验证）
- 可接入自己的 Audiobookshelf 服务器；下载音频后自动转录入库，转录任务可在下载中心查看
- 悬浮字幕支持多句、竖排与外观调整；歌词模式可显示书中插图，宽屏下可交换播放器与正文位置

### 制卡与 Anki

- 新增 **高级制卡**：发送前可按当前内容调整单张卡片的牌组、句子上下文、音频起止与图片
- 视频制卡支持精细预览片段、选择静帧；漫画制卡可将图片裁到句子所在分镜
- 查词弹窗可直接切换制卡 Profile 或牌组
- 支持为兼容的 Anki 笔记模板适配内嵌视频，可撤销适配；需要适配的模板会显示引导
- 桌面端可随 Fushi 启动 Anki；加快片段导出，改善导出字幕原有样式的保留
- 修复 Kiku 模板识别、片段缺少音频、单词音频播放顺序及词典样式导致卡片体积过大等问题

### 视频与字幕

- **图形字幕查词与 OCR**：支持 PGS / VobSub / DVB，可识别当前文字或将整轨转为文本字幕，并覆盖受支持的远程来源
- 视频可按需启用暂停 OCR；识别置信度较低时，可交给已配置的大模型结合上下文重读
- **手机遥控电脑视频**：配对后可浏览字幕、查词、试听句子音频、收藏台词，并让电脑执行制卡
- Windows HDR 视频接入应用内合成，字幕与控制栏可和画面一起显示
- 新增原生蓝光菜单导航、Dolby / DTS 音频直通开关，以及 Atmos / 5.1 / 7.1 标识
- 可同时搜索多个媒体服务器，选择 Emby / Jellyfin 同一条目的不同版本
- 重做播放设置、字幕与音频面板；字幕描边与阴影可自定义，手柄支持暂停句逐词查词
- 支持将音频 CD 导入为 FLAC，支持导入 AIFF 音频
- 修复跳转后字幕位置与动画时间轴、Hi10P 解码选择、硬解画面边缘、蓝光播放及外挂字幕等问题

### 阅读器、漫画与合集

- 小说与漫画新增翻页动效选项；文字高亮支持自定义笔触与扫读动效
- 改善切后台、重新打开及听书跟随后的阅读位置恢复，修复振假名高亮与竖排显示问题
- 重做有声书侧栏、章节导航、阅读设置与工具栏布局编辑器；插图可直接全尺寸打开并双指缩放
- 本地漫画卷、小说与有声书支持自动归入系列合集；合集封面随当前阅读卷更新
- 合集支持筛选、排序与批量操作；番外及未能解析卷序的内容可用 AI 辅助排序
- 本地漫画卷详情新增整卷 OCR、识别进度与书架操作，支持配置跨页偏移与重新打开的位置
- 加快插图加载与长章节打开，修复漫画 OCR 旋转文字和识别区域坐标问题

### AI 与模型管理

- 新增统一的 **「模型」** 设置分类，集中管理大模型、OCR、语音识别、语音合成与模型文件
- 同一提供商支持多个 API Key，加入轮换、冷却、请求限速与临时失败重试
- AI 下载大型系列时改为分批遍历，减少系列内容被截断遗漏的情况
- 关闭 AI 模块后隐藏相关入口并停止 AI 请求，保留已有配置供重新开启

### 同步、桌面与游戏串流

- 内容未变化时跳过重复传输；同步进度显示传输速率，对比页显示漫画页内容
- 覆盖视频与有声书进度前检查冲突；从本机移除的远程书籍可在恢复列表找回
- 可从主机重新获取有声书 / 视频字幕、批量下载合集成员，并在支持时查看或请求主机更新
- 桌面端支持从应用外打开 EPUB / PDF / TXT / 漫画文件；Windows 与 macOS 系统悬浮球新增屏幕 OCR
- 新增桌面命令行 **fushi_cli**，可操作媒体库、播放、查词、制卡、设置、下载与同步
- 游戏串流接收端从 Android 扩展到 iOS、macOS 与 Windows，台词及查词面板可调大小，仍属实验性；串流主机与本机 Galgame Hook 仍仅限 Windows
- 补充 Windows Galgame 引擎适配，包括实验性的 Kogado Hy 与 LucaSystem 适配

### 反馈、统计与浏览器扩展

- 新增 **应用内反馈**：可附日志、截图和视频，保存草稿、查看回复与处理进度；登录后可跨设备查看自己的反馈
- 开发者回复支持通知提醒，iOS 支持推送；已关联修复可查看进入了哪个调试版 / 测试版 / 正式版
- 统计支持自定义日期范围与逐作品明细；修复图表标签、首屏字数及 Galgame 快进 / 历史回看文本误计入阅读的问题
- 浏览器扩展更新 M3E / 玻璃外观；YouTube 待制卡队列可在其他标签页触发生成，修复全屏字幕、缩放、收藏与快捷键等问题

### 升级说明

- 内置复习 **默认关闭**，需要自行开启；在线朗读与 AI 功能需要配置对应服务
- 视频播放已移除「字幕暂停播放模式」（每句字幕结束时自动暂停）
- 极旧的 Hibiki 数据库需先安装 **2.9.1** 并打开一次，再升级至 2.10.0；遇到此情况时应用会提示
- iOS 继续不包含在线扩展源、发现与通用下载中心；游戏串流接收是独立功能
