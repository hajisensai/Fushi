The source code of Fushi is licensed under the GPLv3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)
and [ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md)). The GPLv3 covers copyright
only. **It grants no rights in the name "Fushi", the Fushi logo and app icon,
or the domain "fushi.moe"** (together, the "Fushi Marks"). This policy says how
the Fushi Marks may be used. It exists so that users can always tell whether
they are getting the free, official Fushi.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to Fushi by name in reviews, articles, videos, tutorials or
  discussions, as long as it is accurate and does not suggest endorsement.
- Saying that your project is "based on Fushi" or "a fork of Fushi", as
  required by [ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md) term (c).
- Redistributing an unmodified Official Build under its original name and
  icon, **free of charge**, together with a link to the Official Channels.

## Not allowed without prior written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name "Fushi", with the Fushi logo or
  icon, or with a confusingly similar name, logo or icon.
- Using the Fushi Marks to sell, rent or advertise anything, including in shop
  listings, live streams, "lifetime license" or subscription offers, or devices
  sold with Fushi pre-installed.
- Using the Fushi Marks in a way that suggests you are the Fushi Authors, an
  official partner, or an official distributor.
- Registering a domain, app-store listing, social-media account, company name
  or trademark that contains "Fushi" or a confusingly similar term.

To ask for permission or to report misuse: <contact@fushi.moe>.
